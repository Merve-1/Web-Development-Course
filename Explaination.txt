Hierarchy order is important in SEO

Heading Hierarchy (H1 → H6)
This is the most important on-page hierarchy.

Example structure:
H1 – Main topic of the page (use once)
H2 – Major sections
H3 – Subsections under H2
H4–H6 – Deeper details if needed

Example:
H1: Best SEO Strategies for 2026
H2: On-Page SEO
H3: Keyword Optimization
H3: Internal Linking
H2: Technical SEO
H3: Page Speed
H3: Mobile Optimization

Why it matters:
Helps search engines understand topic importance
Improves content readability
Supports featured snippets

Formatting
- Bold (<b>) – Displays text in bold style to visually highlight it without adding semantic importance.
- Emphasize (<em>) – Emphasizes text to show stress or importance, usually displayed in italics.
- Italic (<i>) – Displays text in italic style for alternate voice, technical terms, or stylistic purposes.
- Strong (<strong>) – Indicates text with strong importance, usually shown in bold and meaningful for accessibility/SEO.
- Insert (<ins>) – Shows text that has been added or inserted into a document (often underlined).
- Delete (<del>) – Indicates text that has been removed from the document (usually shown with a strikethrough).
- Sub (<sub>) – Displays text as subscript, slightly below the normal text line (e.g., chemical formulas like H₂O).
- Sup (<sup>) – Displays text as superscript, slightly above the normal text line (e.g., exponents like x²).
- Mark (<mark>) – Highlights text to show it is relevant or marked for reference.
- Underline (<u>) – Underlines text for stylistic emphasis or to indicate non-textual annotation.
